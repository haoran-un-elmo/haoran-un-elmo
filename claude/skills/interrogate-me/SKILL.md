---
name: interrogate-me
description: Interrogate the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me".
---

Interrogate me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until
we reach a shared understanding. Work to reach clarity and remove
ambiguity. Walk down each branch of the design
tree resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase or reading 
the documentation, explore the codebase instead.

Allow the user to skip a question and defer it to later in the interrogation.

For each question, provide your recommended answer.
